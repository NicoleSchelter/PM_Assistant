**Procurement Management Plan**

**1. Introduction**

**1.1 Purpose**

This Procurement Management Plan establishes the framework for acquiring goods, services, and resources required to support the development and execution of an optimized scheduling solution for the Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor. The plan ensures procurement activities align with project objectives, budget, quality standards, and timeline constraints.

**1.2 Scope**

The procurement scope includes hardware, software, consulting services, and other resources necessary for:

- Developing an AI/ML-driven scheduling system.

- Establishing real-time monitoring infrastructure.

- Supporting research and analysis activities.

- Meeting compliance, security, and stakeholder engagement requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**2. Procurement Approach**

Procurement will be executed in accordance with best practices in vendor management, competitive bidding, and risk mitigation strategies. The procurement process follows the **Plan-Procure-Manage** cycle:

1.  **Planning:** Identify procurement needs, define requirements, and develop specifications.

2.  **Procurement Execution:** Identify vendors, issue RFPs (Request for Proposal), evaluate bids, and select suppliers.

3.  **Contract Management & Monitoring:** Ensure timely delivery, maintain quality control, and enforce contractual obligations.

**2.1 Procurement Methods**

Procurements will follow one of the methods based on the nature of the requirement:

- **Competitive Bidding (RFP/RFQ Process):** Used for software solutions, consultancy services, and technology vendors.

- **Direct Procurement (Single Source Selection):** Applied to specialized requirements such as proprietary railway data systems.

- **Framework Agreements:** For recurring purchases such as cloud infrastructure and hardware components.

- **Internal Procurement:** If NHSRCL or affiliated bodies can provide necessary infrastructure or services.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**3. Procurement Requirements**

The procurement categories align with the project's work breakdown structure (WBS) and include:

**3.1 Software & IT Infrastructure**

- AI-based **timetable optimization software**.

- **Real-time monitoring system** for train operations.

- **Cloud computing services** for data storage and AI model training.

- **Security solutions** ensuring compliance with Indian Railways and international regulations.

**3.2 Hardware Procurement**

- **High-performance servers** with AI processing capabilities.

- **Edge computing devices** for real-time data analysis at railway stations.

- **Backup storage systems** ensuring data redundancy.

- **Network and connectivity hardware** to support data transmission between control centers.

**3.3 Consultancy & Professional Services**

- **Railway scheduling experts** for algorithm validation.

- **AI/ML specialists** for predictive analytics implementation.

- **Cybersecurity consultants** to ensure data protection compliance.

- **Regulatory advisors** for adherence to NHSRCL and international railway standards.

**3.4 Compliance & Quality Assurance Services**

- **Third-party auditors** for compliance with Indian and UIC railway standards.

- **Software testing services** for performance, security, and stress testing.

- **Quality management consultants** to ensure ISO 9001 and CMMI Level 3 adherence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**4. Vendor Selection Criteria**

Vendor selection will be based on:

- **Technical Capability:** Proven expertise in railway scheduling and AI/ML solutions.

- **Regulatory Compliance:** Adherence to NHSRCL, Indian Railway, and international high-speed rail standards.

- **Financial Stability:** Ability to support long-term operations and maintenance.

- **Cost Efficiency:** Competitive pricing with a focus on total cost of ownership (TCO).

- **Service Level Agreements (SLAs):** Guaranteed uptime, support response times, and resolution benchmarks.

The evaluation matrix assigns **weights** to each criterion, ensuring objective vendor selection.

**6. Procurement Risk Management**

**6.1 Key Procurement Risks & Mitigation**

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Risk**                    **Impact**                             **Mitigation Strategy**
  --------------------------- -------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
  Vendor non-performance      Delays in software/hardware delivery   Strict SLAs and performance bonds

  Regulatory non-compliance   Penalties or project delays            Pre-procurement compliance audits

  Budget Overruns             Cost escalation                        Fixed-price contracts where feasible

  Data Security Issues        Compliance violations                  Mandatory security certifications for vendors

  Supply Chain Delays         Hardware shortages                     Diversified vendor sourcing
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**7. Contract Management Strategy**

Contracts will be structured to safeguard project interests through:

- **Fixed-Price Contracts** for clearly defined deliverables (e.g., software licensing).

- **Time & Materials Contracts** for research-based consultancy.

- **Performance-Based Contracts** with incentives for on-time delivery and penalties for non-compliance.

- **Service Level Agreements (SLAs)** defining uptime, response times, and maintenance obligations.

**7.1 Change Management**

- **Change Request Process:** Any procurement-related changes require **formal approvals**.

- **Cost & Impact Assessment:** Changes will be assessed against **budget, timeline, and project scope**.

- **Contract Amendments:** Necessary modifications will be captured in contract addendums.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**8. Procurement Monitoring & Reporting**

Procurement activities will be tracked using a **Procurement Dashboard** linked to project management tools.

**8.1 Performance Metrics**

- **Delivery Timeliness (% of on-time deliveries).**

- **Budget Adherence (% deviation from procurement budget).**

- **Vendor Performance Rating (based on SLA adherence).**

- **Procurement Issue Resolution Time (average time to resolve procurement-related issues).**

**8.2 Procurement Reporting**

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Report Type**             **Frequency**   **Purpose**
  --------------------------- --------------- ------------------------------------------------
  Procurement Status Report   Monthly         Track procurement progress against milestones.

  Vendor Performance Report   Quarterly       Evaluate vendor compliance with SLAs.

  Risk Assessment Report      Bi-Annual       Review procurement-related risks.
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
