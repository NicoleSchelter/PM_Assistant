**Project Roadmap for Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor -- Proposal Development for Efficient Timetable Planning and Execution**

**Phase 1: Project Initiation (Feb 2025)**

- **Key Activities:**

  - Conduct **kickoff meetings** with NHSRCL and stakeholders.

  - Perform **requirements analysis** (align with NHSRCL objectives, evaluate existing studies).

  - Develop a **communication plan** for stakeholder engagement.

  - Define **project scope, objectives, and success factors**.

  - Establish **project schedule and resource allocation**.

- **Milestone:** **Project Kickoff (15th Feb 2025)**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Phase 2: Analysis & Feasibility Study (Feb--Mar 2025)**

- **Key Activities:**

  - Gather **relevant geographic, infrastructure, and timetable data**.

  - Conduct **demand forecasting** (passenger trends, peak hour analysis).

  - Evaluate **technology solutions** (AI/ML scheduling, real-time adjustments).

  - Identify **regulatory constraints** and compliance requirements.

  - Develop **risk register** to assess project risks.

- **Milestone:** **Requirement Analysis Completion (15th Mar 2025)**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Phase 3: Timetable Development & Optimization (Mar--Apr 2025)**

- **Key Activities:**

  - Define **timetable frequency, departure/arrival times**.

  - Perform **simulation-based testing** for feasibility.

  - Optimize **schedules using AI and real-time analytics**.

  - Identify and resolve **bottlenecks in train operations**.

  - Conduct **internal validation and refinement**.

- **Milestone:** **Draft Proposal Submission (30th Apr 2025)**

**Phase 4: Proposal Refinement & Quality Assurance (May 2025)**

- **Key Activities:**

  - Conduct **stakeholder review sessions** with NHSRCL.

  - Implement **feedback and refinements** in the proposal.

  - Perform **quality assurance reviews** (accuracy, compliance checks).

  - Finalize **cost estimates and ROI projections**.

  - Develop **presentation materials for NHSRCL approval**.

- **Milestone:** **Proposal Review and Refinement (31st May 2025)**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Phase 5: Final Submission & Approval Process (Jun 2025)**

- **Key Activities:**

  - Present **final proposal** to NHSRCL Steering Committee.

  - Address **last-minute feedback or compliance concerns**.

  - Obtain **formal approval from NHSRCL and Project Sponsor**.

  - Conduct **lessons learned session** for future improvements.

- **Milestone:** **Final Proposal Submission (15th Jun 2025)**
