**Requirements Management Plan**

**Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor -- Scheduling Optimization**

**1. Introduction**

The **Requirements Management Plan** defines the processes and methodologies for identifying, documenting, tracking, validating, and managing changes to project requirements. This plan ensures that all stakeholders align with the project objectives and that the requirements remain clear, complete, and traceable throughout the project lifecycle.

The objective of this plan is to:

- Ensure that all functional and non-functional requirements are properly elicited, documented, and maintained.

- Provide a framework for tracking and managing requirements throughout the project.

- Establish processes for validating and reporting requirements to stakeholders.

- Define the roles, responsibilities, and authority levels for requirement-related decisions.

**2. Requirements Development**

**2.1 Process for Eliciting Requirements**

The following techniques will be used for gathering and refining project requirements:

- **Stakeholder Interviews & Workshops**: Regular meetings with NHSRCL teams, government regulatory bodies, and technical vendors to identify needs.

- **Document Analysis**: Review of existing operational guidelines, railway safety regulations, and best practices from international high-speed rail systems.

- **Surveys & Questionnaires**: Collect insights from passengers and operational staff.

- **Prototyping & Simulations**: Use AI-driven timetable simulations to refine operational feasibility.

- **Use Case Development**: Define real-world scenarios for scheduling, conflict resolution, and emergency handling.

**2.2 Requirements Documentation**

All requirements will be documented in the **System Requirements Specification (SRS)** and maintained in a **requirements repository**. Documentation includes:

- **Unique Requirement ID** for traceability.

- **Category** (functional, non-functional, technical, regulatory).

- **Description** with business justification.

- **Priority Level** (high, medium, low).

- **Source** (stakeholder, regulation, historical data).

- **Acceptance Criteria** to validate implementation.

- **Change History** for tracking modifications.

**3. Requirements Tracking and Management**

**3.1 Tracking Requirements**

A **Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)** will be used to track requirements from their origin to implementation and testing. The RTM will:

- Link each requirement to its source, priority, and implementation status.

- Map requirements to project deliverables.

- Identify dependencies between requirements.

- Track testing results to verify compliance.

**3.2 Managing Changes to Requirements**

All changes to requirements must follow a structured **Change Control Process**:

1.  **Change Request Submission**: Stakeholders submit change requests with justification and impact assessment.

2.  **Impact Analysis**: The project team evaluates changes in terms of scope, cost, schedule, and risk.

3.  **Approval Process**: Change requests are reviewed by the **Change Control Board (CCB)**.

4.  **Update Documentation**: Approved changes are incorporated into project documents and communicated to stakeholders.

5.  **Version Control**: Maintain a historical record of changes in the requirements repository.

**4. Requirements Validation and Reporting**

**4.1 Validation Process**

The following validation techniques will ensure requirements meet project needs:

- **Prototyping & User Testing**: Validate scheduling algorithms through real-world simulations.

- **Stakeholder Review Sessions**: Regular review meetings with NHSRCL to ensure alignment.

- **Automated System Testing**: AI-driven validation of scheduling system performance.

- **Compliance Audits**: Ensure adherence to **Indian Railways Regulations, UIC Standards, and ISO 9001:2015**.

**4.2 Reporting Requirements**

Requirement status reports will be shared with stakeholders at defined intervals. The reports will include:

- **Status Dashboard** (approved, in progress, pending approval).

- **Change Log** with justifications and impact analysis.

- **Compliance Reports** to ensure regulatory adherence.

- **Issue Tracker** for unresolved requirement conflicts.

**5. Roles and Responsibilities**

**5.1 Stakeholder Roles**

- **Project Manager (Roger Candoo)**: Oversees requirements management activities.

- **NHSRCL Representatives**: Provide operational insights and validate requirements.

- **Technical Team**: Implement scheduling algorithms and ensure compliance.

- **Quality Assurance Team**: Conduct validation and audits.

- **Change Control Board (CCB)**: Review and approve requirement changes.

**5.2 Responsibilities**

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Role**                 **Responsibility**
  ------------------------ -------------------------------------------------------------------
  Project Manager          Ensures all requirements align with project scope and objectives.

  NHSRCL Representatives   Provide domain expertise and regulatory guidance.

  Development Team         Implement requirements and ensure functionality.

  QA Team                  Verify that requirements meet defined acceptance criteria.

  CCB                      Evaluate and approve requirement changes.
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**6. Authorization and Decision-Making**

**6.1 Decision-Making Process**

Decisions regarding requirements follow this structured process:

- Minor changes (**low impact**) are approved by the **Project Manager**.

- Medium impact changes require **CCB approval**.

- High-impact changes affecting scope, budget, or compliance require **NHSRCL approval**.

**6.2 Authorization Levels**

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Approval Level**           **Required For**
  ---------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Project Manager              Minor adjustments (documentation updates, minor technical refinements).

  Change Control Board (CCB)   Functional requirement modifications, scheduling system changes.

  NHSRCL Approval              Regulatory compliance changes, budget-impacting changes.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**7. Requirements Prioritization**

**7.1 Prioritization Criteria**

Requirements will be prioritized based on:

- **Regulatory Compliance** (mandatory adherence to railway safety and operational standards).

- **Operational Impact** (direct effect on punctuality, efficiency, and passenger satisfaction).

- **Technical Feasibility** (ease of implementation and system constraints).

- **Financial Constraints** (budget considerations).

**7.2 Prioritization Process**

A **MoSCoW Method** will be used:

- **Must-Have** -- Essential for project success.

- **Should-Have** -- Important but not critical.

- **Could-Have** -- Desirable but not urgent.

- **Won't-Have** -- Deferred for future phases.

**8. Metrics and Traceability**

**8.1 Metrics**

Key metrics to evaluate requirements management effectiveness:

- **Requirements Stability Index**: (Approved requirements - Changed requirements) / Total requirements.

- **Change Request Impact Score**: Measure of requirement changes affecting cost/schedule.

- **Validation Coverage**: % of requirements validated through testing.

- **Stakeholder Satisfaction Score**: Feedback from NHSRCL representatives.
