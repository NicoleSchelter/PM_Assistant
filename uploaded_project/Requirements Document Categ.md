# Requirements Document

**Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor**\
*Development of an Optimized Scheduling Solution*

## 1. Business Requirements

- **Objective:** To design, develop, and implement an optimized scheduling solution for the Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor to enhance efficiency and punctuality.

- **Strategic Goals:**

  - Align with the National High-Speed Rail Corporation Limited (NHSRCL) goals for operational excellence.

  - Optimize resource allocation and reduce train delays.

  - Improve passenger experience with accurate, real-time scheduling.

  - Ensure compliance with national and international railway regulations.

- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

  - Train punctuality improvement by 20%.

  - System uptime of 99.99%.

  - 95% scheduling accuracy in predictive modeling.

## 2. Stakeholder Requirements

- **Internal Stakeholders:**

  - NHSRCL Operations Team: Oversee train scheduling and operations.

  - Maintenance Department: Ensure integration of maintenance schedules.

  - IT Department: Support system integration and security.

- **External Stakeholders:**

  - Passengers: Depend on real-time schedule updates.

  - Government regulatory bodies: Require compliance with safety and operational regulations.

  - Technology vendors: Provide infrastructure and support for implementation.

- **Engagement Plan:**

  - Regular NHSRCL stakeholder meetings.

  - Training sessions for system adoption.

  - Public awareness campaigns on scheduling updates.

## 3. Functional Requirements

- **Scheduling System:**

  - Automatic train schedule generation based on predefined operational parameters.

  - Real-time adjustment for delays, maintenance, and emergencies.

  - Optimization based on passenger demand, station capacity, travel time, and energy consumption.

- **Operational Planning:**

  - Efficient allocation of routes to minimize conflicts.

  - Dynamic resource allocation for rolling stock and crew.

  - Maintenance integration for predictive repairs and servicing.

- **Real-Time Monitoring and Control:**

  - Dynamic delay management and mitigation strategies.

  - Live updates on train positioning and punctuality performance.

  - Decision-support insights for operational staff.

- **User Access & Security:**

  - Role-based access control for operators, managers, and administrators.

  - User-friendly interface for intuitive operation.

  - Multi-factor authentication for critical user roles.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Performance:**

  - The system must support up to 200 trains per day.

  - Low-latency updates within 2 seconds for real-time data.

- **Security:**

  - Data encryption for secure storage and transmission.

  - Comprehensive audit logging of system activities.

- **Regulatory Compliance:**

  - Adhere to Indian Railways' safety and operational regulations.

  - Comply with UIC international high-speed rail standards.

- **Maintainability:**

  - Modular system architecture to facilitate updates and troubleshooting.

  - Extensive documentation and training materials for NHSRCL staff.

## 5. Transition Requirements

- **Data Migration:**

  - Integrate with NHSRCL's existing IT infrastructure.

  - Support real-time data feeds from weather data, passenger information, and signaling systems.

- **Training & Adoption:**

  - Conduct onboarding sessions for NHSRCL staff.

  - Provide user manuals and operational guides.

- **Implementation Phases:**

  - **Phase 1:** Initial development and testing (12 months).

  - **Phase 2:** System validation and pilot testing (6 months).

  - **Phase 3:** Full-scale implementation (within 18 months from project initiation).

- **Change Management:**

  - Address resistance from operational teams through workshops and support sessions.

  - Conduct stakeholder engagement to ensure system acceptance.

## 6. Assumptions and Constraints

- Availability of accurate passenger and operational data.

- Continuous funding and resource allocation.

- NHSRCL's active collaboration during requirement gathering and system integration.

## 7. Risks and Mitigation Strategies

- **Risk: Data inaccuracies affecting scheduling accuracy.**

  - *Mitigation:* Regular data validation and audit processes.

- **Risk: Project milestones being delayed.**

  - *Mitigation:* Robust project management with contingency planning.

- **Risk: Resistance from operational staff in adopting the system.**

  - *Mitigation:* Change management initiatives, stakeholder engagement, and hands-on training sessions.

## 8. Deliverables

- **Documentation:**

  - System Requirement Specification (SRS).

  - User training materials and manuals.

- **Software Deliverables:**

  - Scheduling and real-time monitoring application.

  - API integrations for external system connectivity.

- **Training & Support:**

  - Instructor-led and self-paced training programs for NHSRCL teams.

## 9. Acceptance Criteria

- System must meet all functional and non-functional requirements.

- A successful pilot run with a subset of operations.

- Formal sign-off from NHSRCL project team confirming system readiness.
