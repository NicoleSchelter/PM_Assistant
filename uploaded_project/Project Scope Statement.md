**Project Scope Statement**

**Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor -- Proposal Development for Efficient Timetable Planning and Execution**

**1. Project Objectives**

The primary objective of this project is to develop a **comprehensive, cost-effective, and data-driven proposal** for the planning and execution of train schedules along the Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor. This proposal will aim to:

- **Enhance efficiency and punctuality** of train operations using AI/ML-based predictive analytics.

- **Ensure compliance** with NHSRCL guidelines and international railway standards.

- **Optimize resource allocation** to improve operational efficiency and minimize delays.

- **Deliver a high-quality proposal** that includes technical feasibility analysis, financial projections, and an implementation roadmap for NHSRCL.

**2. Project Scope Description**

The project involves the development of a detailed **timetable planning and execution proposal** that incorporates:

- **Automated scheduling models** that optimize train operations based on passenger demand, station capacity, and energy efficiency.

- **Real-time monitoring and adjustment mechanisms** for delay management and dynamic schedule updates.

- **Stakeholder collaboration** with NHSRCL and regulatory bodies to align the proposal with operational needs.

- **Financial analysis** including cost estimates, return on investment (ROI), and funding requirements.

- **Regulatory compliance framework** to ensure adherence to Indian Railways' safety and operational guidelines.

**In-Scope:**

- AI/ML-driven timetable modeling and optimization.

- Compliance assessment against Indian and international railway standards.

- Proposal documentation, including technical details and cost estimates.

- Development of presentation materials for NHSRCL stakeholders.

**Out-of-Scope:**

- Direct implementation of the scheduling system.

- Development of hardware infrastructure for railway operations.

- Deployment of AI/ML models in live railway operations.

**3. Project Deliverables**

The key deliverables for this project include:

- **Proposal Document:**

  - Executive summary and strategic objectives.

  - Detailed project scope, methodology, and implementation roadmap.

  - Cost-benefit analysis and ROI projections.

- **Technical Solution:**

  - AI/ML-driven timetable optimization framework.

  - System architecture for real-time monitoring.

  - Simulation models for schedule feasibility validation.

- **Presentation Materials:**

  - PowerPoint presentations for NHSRCL stakeholders.

  - Supporting documents, graphs, and data visualizations.

**4. Acceptance Criteria**

For the proposal and deliverables to be accepted, the following conditions must be met:

- The proposal aligns with NHSRCL's strategic goals and operational requirements.

- AI-driven scheduling models achieve at least **95% accuracy** in simulations.

- Compliance with NHSRCL and **international high-speed rail standards (UIC, ISO 9001:2015)**.

- **Stakeholder approval** from NHSRCL's review committee.

**5. Project Constraints**

- **Time:** The final proposal must be submitted by **June 15, 2025**.

- **Budget:** Total budget is estimated at **€950,000**, with allocated costs for personnel, R&D, and documentation.

- **Regulatory Compliance:** The solution must meet **Indian Railways' safety and operational regulations**.

- **Technology:** AI/ML models must be compatible with NHSRCL's existing IT infrastructure.

**6. Project Assumptions**

- NHSRCL will provide **timely access to operational data** required for timetable modeling.

- The project team will have access to **sufficient computational resources** for AI/ML simulations.

- Key stakeholders will be available for **collaboration and review meetings** as scheduled.

- The **budget and funding** for proposal development will remain unchanged throughout the project duration.

**7. Project Exclusions**

The project does not include:

- Physical construction or modification of railway infrastructure.

- Deployment of scheduling models into NHSRCL's operational system.

- Real-time AI testing with actual train movements.

**8. Project Boundaries**

The project will start with a **requirement analysis phase** (Feb 2025) and conclude with the **submission of the final proposal (June 2025)**. Key boundary conditions include:

- **Geographical scope:** The Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor.

- **Operational focus:** Timetable planning and execution, not railway maintenance or infrastructure development.
