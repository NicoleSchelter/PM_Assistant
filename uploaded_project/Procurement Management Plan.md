**Procurement Management Plan**

**Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor -- Timetable Optimization System**

**1. Introduction**

**1.1 Project Overview**

The Mumbai--Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor aims to enhance rail operations through an AI-driven timetable optimization system. The project will develop an advanced scheduling solution that integrates predictive analytics, real-time adjustments, and compliance with railway standards.

**1.2 Purpose of the Procurement Management Plan**

This plan ensures that all procurement activities align with project goals, are cost-effective, and meet quality standards. It provides a structured approach for acquiring essential resources while mitigating risks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**2. Procurement Management Approach**

**2.1 Procurement Integration with Project Planning**

Procurement activities are aligned with the project schedule, ensuring that key components are available as needed. The procurement process integrates with:

- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)** -- to determine procurement dependencies.

- **Project Schedule** -- to align procurement with implementation milestones.

- **Risk Management Plan** -- to mitigate procurement risks such as delays or non-compliance.

**2.2 Coordination with Project Stakeholders**

Procurement is managed collaboratively with NHSRCL, technical teams, and vendors to ensure seamless integration into existing infrastructure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**3. Procurement Process**

**3.1 Identifying Procurement Needs**

The **High-Performance Cloud Server** is critical for supporting real-time AI computations. The procurement process includes:

- Identifying technical specifications.

- Analyzing compatibility with existing NHSRCL IT infrastructure.

- Ensuring vendor compliance with Indian railway cybersecurity regulations.

**5. Procurement Requirements**

**5.1 High-Performance Cloud Server Specifications**

- **Processor:** Minimum 32-core CPU (AMD EPYC or Intel Xeon)

- **RAM:** 256 GB minimum

- **Storage:** 2 TB SSD + scalable cloud storage

- **Network:** 10 Gbps bandwidth for real-time data processing

- **Security:** Compliance with Indian data protection laws

- **Uptime:** 99.99% availability

**5.2 Quality Requirements**

- Must support **real-time AI-based schedule adjustments**.

- Integration with **existing NHSRCL railway control systems**.

- **Data encryption** and **secure API connectivity**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**6. Vendor Management**

**6.1 Vendor Selection Criteria**

- Proven experience in providing cloud infrastructure for railway or government projects.

- Compliance with **ISO 27001** and **Indian IT Act**.

- Minimum **99.99% server uptime guarantee**.

- Established **local support team in India**.

**6.2 Vendor Evaluation Process**

1.  **Shortlisting:** Identify at least **three vendors**.

2.  **Technical Review:** Validate compliance with NHSRCL requirements.

3.  **Financial Review:** Assess total cost of ownership (TCO) and service-level agreements (SLAs).

4.  **Final Selection:** Conduct negotiations and sign a contract.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**7. Procurement Documentation**

**7.1 Types of Procurement Documents**

- **Request for Proposal (RFP)** -- Details technical and quality requirements.

- **Request for Quotation (RFQ)** -- Requests pricing and availability from vendors.

- **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** -- Defines uptime, support, and maintenance terms.

**7.2 Bid Document Preparation and Submission**

- The RFP will be issued by **15th March 2025**.

- Vendors must submit proposals by **30th March 2025**.

- Evaluation and final selection by **15th April 2025**.
